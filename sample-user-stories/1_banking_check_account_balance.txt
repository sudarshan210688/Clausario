Check Account Balance

As a bank customer
I want to view my real-time account balance
So that I know my available funds before making decisions

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given I'm logged in, when I open the "Accounts" tab, then my current balance and available balance (post-holds) are displayed
- Given I have multiple accounts, when I view the dashboard, then balances for all linked accounts are shown separately
- Given the balance fails to load, when there's a network/service error, then a retry option and last-synced timestamp are shown instead of a blank/incorrect value
