Block/Freeze Debit Card

As a bank customer
I want to instantly block my debit card from the app
So that I can prevent fraud if my card is lost or stolen

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given I select a card, when I tap "Block Card" and confirm, then the card is deactivated within seconds and no further transactions are approved
- Given a card is blocked, when I view my card list, then its status clearly shows "Blocked" with a timestamp
- Given a card is blocked, when I want to use it again, then I can request a replacement or (if temp-locked) unblock it via OTP verification
